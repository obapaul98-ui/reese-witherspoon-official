About Reese

Born to Tell Stories

Born in New Orleans on March 22, 1976 and raised in Nashville, Tennessee, Laura Jeanne Reese Witherspoon made her screen debut at just 14 years old. Decades later, she stands as one of the most decorated and influential women in Hollywood history.

Her 2006 Oscar for Walk the Line, Emmy for Big Little Lies, and the founding of Hello Sunshine are just the headline chapters of an extraordinary life lived on her own terms — always putting women's stories first.
